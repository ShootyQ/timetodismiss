Frequently asked

How long does setup take?

Most schools are up and running in under an hour. Import rosters from CSV, generate QR tags, and you’re ready for first dismissal.

Do we need special hardware?

No. Any modern browser works. Bluetooth barcode scanners are optional — you can also scan using a device camera.

Can we call siblings or carpools together?

Yes. Group students into a ride share and call the entire group at once from the page or by scanning their tag.

How does the classroom display update?

When a caller selects or scans a tag, every relevant classroom page updates instantly. Teachers see new names highlighted at the top.
