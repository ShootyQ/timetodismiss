Frequently asked

How long does setup take?

Most schools are up and running in under an hour. Import rosters, generate QR tags, and you’re ready for first dismissal.

Do we need special hardware?

No. Any modern browser works. Bluetooth scanners are optional — device cameras can scan too.

Can we call siblings or carpools together?

Yes. Group students into a ride share and call the entire group at once from the page or by scanning their tag.

How does the classroom display update?

Calls instantly update every relevant classroom page. New names are highlighted at the top.
