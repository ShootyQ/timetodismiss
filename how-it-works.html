How It Works

Time To Dismiss keeps pick-up calm and coordinated. Here's a quick overview of how callers, classrooms, and the live display work together.

Setup

1) Add your school

Add your school and basic details. You can import rosters via CSV.

2) Add your school info

Add classes, students, and ride shares. Group siblings or shared rides under a single ride share name; each group can have multiple tags.

3) Print QR codes

Generate QR codes (with human-readable tags) sized for common label sheets.

Calling

Start a Dismissal

Kick off a dismissal session in one click. The master page keeps the flow organized and updates every classroom live.

Call Students — Page or Scan

Find and call students from the page with search and class filters, or scan a QR tag using a Bluetooth barcode scanner or your device camera — fast, simple, and no extra setup.

Ride Shares & Family Calls

Group siblings and carpools under a ride share name, then call the whole group at once — mobile‑friendly for on‑the‑go calling.

In the Classroom

Live Class View

The class page shows a clean, projector-friendly list of students. As calls come in, students are highlighted so they can line up calmly.

Device-Friendly

Works on TVs, projectors, laptops, and tablets. Large type and high contrast for visibility from across the room.
