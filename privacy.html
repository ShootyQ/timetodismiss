Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2025

Time To Dismiss (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides tools to streamline student dismissal. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account information (staff email, display name) when you sign in with your school account.
  • School and class data configured by administrators (class names, car groups, rosters).
  • Operational data required for the service (e.g., student status updates: waiting, called, picked up).
  • Device/usage data such as browser type and approximate region (via standard server logs).

How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the service, including real-time classroom updates.
  • Secure authentication and role-based access (viewer, caller, admin).
  • Improve reliability, performance, and support.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers (e.g., hosting and authentication) who process it on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and security obligations, and as required by law.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including encryption in transit and role-based access. No system is 100% secure; please use strong, school-managed accounts.

Data Retention

Operational data is retained while your school uses the service or as required for legitimate business or legal purposes. Administrators may request deletion of school data.

Children’s Privacy

Students do not create accounts. The service displays limited student information for operational purposes to authorized staff only.

Your Choices

  • Administrators control school, class, and roster data.
  • Staff can request access changes through their school administrator.
  • For questions or data requests, visit our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our practices. We will revise the date above and, when appropriate, notify administrators.
